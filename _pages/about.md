## Bios

I am currently a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Michigan State University, working with [Prof. Guo-Wei Wei](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/weig/) in East Lansing, Michigan. I earned my Ph.D. in Materials Physics and Chemistry from Peking University in 2022 under the supervision of [Prof. Feng Pan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OZJPZK8AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). 
<!-- Prior to that, I completed my B.E. degree with honors in Theoretical Physics from Ningbo University in 2017. -->

**Research Interests:** My research lies in the field of **AI for Science**, with a particular focus on integrating mathematical modeling and AI to advance chemical, materials, and biological sciences. I work on **Topological Data Analysis (TDA)**, manifold learning, and higher-order structures. I also develop AI models for computational biology, molecular prediction, and materials discovery, with applications in drug design, addiction research, batteries, and Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs).
